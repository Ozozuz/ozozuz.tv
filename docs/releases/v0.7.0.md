# v0.7.0 — Persistent storage for an increasingly volatile memory

- Added a manual **Già vista** checkbox below the episode player, saved locally across app restarts and catalogue-domain changes.
- Watched episodes get a small green tick on their preview thumbnail. Unchecking the box removes it.
- Added **Scarica ↓**: press Play once, choose a local folder and download a complete MP4 with audio. The app prefers quality up to 720p and Italian audio when available.
- Background progress and cancellation, partial-file cleanup, and **Apri MP4** for native local playback. One download at a time; subtitles are not exported and interrupted downloads restart.
- Online playback restrictions remain in place. The download button does not send you to an external page full of alternative life choices.

Android 8.0+, versionCode 11. Same package and development signer for upgrades.

Validation: 32 JVM tests, Android SDK build and APK signing checks passed. Real manifests and one decrypted video/audio segment were inspected (H.264/AAC); a full Android download and local playback have not been tested on a phone.
