# ozozuz.tv 0.9.0

Your screen-rotation lock has been relieved of its video-related duties.

- **↻** forces landscape for online and downloaded videos, even with device
  auto-rotate disabled. Back restores the previous orientation.
- Google Cast device picker, send-current-video button, mini/expanded controls
  and notifications. Supports online HLS and locally downloaded MP4s through
  a temporary receiver-only Wi-Fi relay.
- Phone and receiver need the same IPv4 Wi-Fi and Google Play Services on the
  sender. The phone must remain connected. No transcoding or native AirPlay/DLNA.
- Same preview package and signer as 0.8.0; update preserves preview app data.

Physical phone/Chromecast verification is still required. Compilation and unit
results are recorded in VALIDATION.md. No emulator was started.
